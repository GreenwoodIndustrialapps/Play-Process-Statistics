Overview

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Play Process Statistics ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Collection

Play Process Statistics does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Your Play Creates

The times of your last 25 runs, your personal best, your sound setting and which coach question you were last shown are stored locally on your device only. This data is never uploaded, synced to a server, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Device Permissions

Play Process Statistics does not request access to your camera, microphone, speech recognition, photo library, location, contacts, or any other protected part of your device.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks your own results is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment details.

Third-Party Services

Play Process Statistics does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Play Process Statistics does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, as no data is collected at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
