About the App

Play Process Statistics is a short tap game that lets you feel what process statistics mean. Each run is 30 cycles: tap the green part, avoid the red one, and keep a steady pace. When the run ends, your results show your Cpk, your OEE, a histogram and control chart of your own times, and a history of your runs.

A coach question after each run asks you something about what just happened, and the Stat Guide explains the ideas in plain language. Everything in the game is a teaching example. See the Disclaimer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What iOS version do I need?

Play Process Statistics requires iOS 16.6 or later, on iPhone.

My average is on target, so why is my Cpk low?

Cpk looks at both where your times are centred and how widely they spread. A steady rhythm gives a narrow spread and a higher Cpk, even if you are a little off target, as long as you stay well inside the limits. A wide spread pushes Cpk down however good the average is.

Why does a stop not count in Cpk?

A stop is a breakdown, not how your process normally runs, so it is left out of Cpk. It still costs you OEE, and it still shows on the histogram and the control chart.

What is free, and what needs the unlock?

You can play as much as you like for free, and the Results page shows an example run. A single one-time purchase unlocks your own results, your history, your personal best and the coach questions. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Results, tap Unlock, then Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. It also unlocks by itself after a reinstall.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No data is collected or transmitted. Your last 25 runs and your personal best stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I reset my personal best?

Yes. On Results, in History, tap Reset next to Personal best, then confirm.

I cannot hear any sound.

Check the red speaker button on the Play page is not muted, and that your phone volume is up.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email support using the button above. If you're reporting an issue, include your device model and iOS version.
