A career in quality

I've spent 35 years in quality, Six Sigma, and Lean within automotive manufacturing, and one lesson keeps coming back: you can read a Cpk or an OEE figure without ever feeling what moves it. A chart can tell you a process is capable, but it rarely shows what a rushed pace, an uneven rhythm or one stoppage does to the numbers.

The gap

These ideas are usually taught with formulas and tables. Working through them on paper explains the sums but not the feel, and it was never something you could pick up for a minute and come back to. I wanted people to play a short run and then see the same ideas in their own results.

Why Play Process Statistics

So I built it, as a companion to Process Capability Calculator App, Scatter Plot and Rolled Throughput Yield. A run is 30 cycles of tapping: hit the green part, avoid the red one, and keep a steady pace. Go too fast or too slow and your average drifts. Be uneven and your spread grows. Make a mistake and you get a bad part or a stop.

Your results then show your Cpk and OEE, a histogram and control chart of your own times, and a history of your runs. A coach question after each run asks you something about what just happened, and the Stat Guide explains the ideas in plain language.

Everything runs entirely on your own device. Nothing from your play ever leaves your phone.
